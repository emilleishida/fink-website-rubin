Over the years, artists in and around the Fink collaboration have created posters, illustrations, mascots and other visuals that gave Fink its own identity beyond the science. This is a growing collection of that artwork, organised into a few sections below — pick one to browse.

The images are compressed so the pages stay light. Click on any piece to see it a little larger, read a short description, and find a link to the full-resolution file.
